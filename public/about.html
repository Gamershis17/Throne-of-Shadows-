THRONE OF SHADOWS

Tap. Loot. Rebirth. Offline progress.

⚔️ The Story

Throne of Shadows began as a simple idea: a game you can play anywhere, anytime — no downloads, no installs, just open your browser and fight.

Set in a dark fantasy realm, you are a hero rising through the shadows. Battle monsters, collect legendary gear, tame powerful pets, and climb from a nameless wanderer to the ruler of the Throne itself.

Every tap matters. Every victory brings you closer to godhood.

🎮 How to Play

  • ⚔️ Battle — Tap to attack enemies. Defeat bosses to advance to harder stages.
  • 🎒 Gear Up — Loot weapons, armor, and accessories. Enchant them to +10 for massive power boosts.
  • 🐾 Pets — Tame and train pets that fight alongside you with their own HP and abilities.
  • 🌳 Talents — Unlock powerful talent trees for your class. Earn points as you level up.
  • 🔄 Rebirth — Reset your progress for permanent bonuses. Rise stronger each time.
  • 🏰 Guilds — Team up with other players, donate to your guild hall, and unlock perks.
  • 🎣 Fishing — Cast your line in the Lake of Whispers. Sell, eat, or feed your catches to pets.
  • ⛏️ Mining — Break rocks for valuable ores and materials.
